Public copy — footer, Terms, Privacy, Cookie Notice, Account Agreement, Driver Disclosure — uses: “Detours Fleet Ops is a registered business name of SRV Freight Inc.” The contracting party is “SRV Freight Inc. o/a Detours Fleet Ops.” Do not publish the BIN or corporation number on the site.
